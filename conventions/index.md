# Conventions

The standards and formats this knowledge repository is built on.

* [OKF](./okf/index.md) - the Open Knowledge Format: the specification, authoring guide, and validation tooling every concept here follows.

# Authoring

How to shape and word the knowledge itself, on top of the OKF mechanics.

* [Describe the present, not the change](./describe-current-state.md) - concept pages state what a subject is now, never the change or project that produced it.
* [Organize by product, not technology](./organize-by-product.md) - group concepts around what a reader perceives, not the layer that implements it.
* [Layout of a project area](./project-area-layout.md) - the shape every project takes: a product-description root plus one folder per exposure surface.
* [Write in American English](./american-english.md) - the whole repository is written in American English; "color", not "colour".
