# Learning

Structured, deliberate self-study. Where [Engineering](../engineering/index.md)
captures knowledge that is already load-bearing, Learning captures skills being
*actively acquired* — the reason for learning them, the trusted resources feeding
that learning, the lessons worked through, and the records of what stuck.

Each learning area follows a small, repeatable shape:

* a **mission** — the real-world reason the skill is being learned, which grounds
  everything else;
* **resources** — the high-trust books, talks, and communities the knowledge is
  drawn from;
* a **glossary** and other **references** — the compressed, durable units of
  knowledge, meant to be revisited;
* **lessons** — self-contained units of teaching, each a tangible win;
* **learning records** — dated notes on what was learned or decided, used to judge
  what to teach next.
