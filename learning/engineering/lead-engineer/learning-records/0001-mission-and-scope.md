# Context

First session of the lead engineer track. Establishing why it exists and how it
will be taught, so future sessions can judge the zone of proximal development.

# What was decided

* **Mission framing:** the learner is a senior IC **aiming for a lead
  promotion** — the goal is both *skills* and *evidence*, via the strategy of
  [operating one scope up](../glossary.md) before holding the title. See
  [mission](../mission.md).
* **Scope:** all four dimensions are in — technical direction, people &
  mentoring, delivery & execution, influence & communication. Breadth over depth
  initially, because a lead is judged across all four.
* **Format:** hybrid, but everything in the vault. Durable knowledge as OKF
  Markdown; interactive lessons as HTML under `lessons/`. speki only validates
  Markdown, so HTML in the bundle is fine.
* **Framework of record:** Larson's four [Staff archetypes](../glossary.md) as
  the map of the role; Reilly's three pillars as the skills model. These are the
  spine of the track.

# Starting point (for judging ZPD)

* Strong hands-on engineer; new to *naming and deliberately practising* the
  leadership scope. Lessons should bridge from concrete engineering to leadership
  behaviours.
* No confirmed employer promotion rubric yet — a known gap and the highest-value
  resource to obtain.

# Open questions / assumptions to confirm

* Target is a technical-track tech-lead/staff role at the current employer
  (assumed, not confirmed).
* No fixed promotion deadline (assumed).
* Which archetype fits the learner's current environment best — likely **Tech
  Lead** as the on-ramp, but to be confirmed as we learn more.
