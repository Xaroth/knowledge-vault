# Learning Records

Dated notes capturing what was learned or decided in the [lead engineer](../index.md)
track — loosely equivalent to architectural decision records. Each record captures
a non-obvious lesson or key insight that may need to be revised later, or that
drives a future session. They are the primary input for judging what to teach next.

Records are numbered and append-only; supersede rather than delete.
