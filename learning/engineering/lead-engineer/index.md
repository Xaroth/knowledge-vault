# Lead Engineer

Becoming an effective technical leader. This track treats *lead engineer* as a
scope of impact rather than a job title: the shift from being responsible for your
own work to being responsible for the technical success of a team and its
surroundings — spanning technical direction, growing other engineers, delivery,
and cross-team influence.

The track is taught in short lessons, each a single tangible win grounded in the
[mission](./mission.md), and backed by durable references that are meant to be
revisited long after the lessons are forgotten.
