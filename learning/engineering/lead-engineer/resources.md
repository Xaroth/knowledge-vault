Curated, high-trust sources this track draws on. Lessons cite these rather than
asserting from memory. Marked **★** are the primary sources — start there.

# Books

* **★ The Staff Engineer's Path** — Tanya Reilly (O'Reilly, 2022). The clearest
  single manual for the technical-leadership-without-management track. Built on
  three pillars: **big-picture thinking**, **execution**, and **being a role
  model / levelling up others**. Maps almost perfectly onto this track's four
  dimensions. <https://www.oreilly.com/library/view/the-staff-engineers/9781098118723/>
* **Staff Engineer: Leadership beyond the management track** — Will Larson
  (2021). Origin of the four archetypes; strong on getting promoted to and
  operating at Staff+. Companion site with free guides and real-world stories:
  <https://staffeng.com/>
* **The Manager's Path** — Camille Fournier (O'Reilly, 2017). The "Tech Lead"
  and "Managing a Team" chapters are essential even on a non-management track;
  the canonical description of the tech-lead role.
  <https://www.oreilly.com/library/view/the-managers-path/9781491973882/>
* **An Elegant Puzzle** — Will Larson. Systems thinking for engineering orgs;
  useful for the delivery and org-design dimensions. <https://lethain.com/elegant-puzzle/>
* **Team Topologies** — Matthew Skelton & Manuel Pais. Vocabulary for team
  boundaries, cognitive load, and interaction modes — core to influence and
  technical direction across teams. <https://teamtopologies.com/>
* **Turn the Ship Around!** — L. David Marquet. Leadership as distributing
  intent and authority; a readable model for leading without hierarchy.

# Newsletters & blogs

* **★ The Pragmatic Engineer** — Gergely Orosz. Deeply-sourced, real-world
  engineering-career and leadership writing. <https://newsletter.pragmaticengineer.com/>
* **Irrational Exuberance** — Will Larson's blog; source of the
  [archetypes essay](https://lethain.com/staff-engineer-archetypes/).
* **LeadDev** — articles, talks, and conferences focused on engineering
  leadership. <https://leaddev.com/>
* **StaffEng stories** — first-person accounts of reaching Staff+, useful for
  seeing what an evidenced promotion case looks like. <https://staffeng.com/stories/>

# Canonical essays & references

* **Staff archetypes** — Will Larson. The Tech Lead / Architect / Solver / Right
  Hand map. <https://staffeng.com/guides/staff-archetypes/>
* **Google Engineering Practices** — code review standards and author/reviewer
  guides; a concrete reference for "code review as teaching."
  <https://google.github.io/eng-practices/>

# Communities (for wisdom — testing skills with peers)

* **Rands Leadership Slack** — large, active community of engineering leaders and
  aspiring leaders. <https://randsinrepose.com/welcome-to-rands-leadership-slack/>
* **r/ExperiencedDevs** — candid discussion of senior/lead career questions.
  <https://www.reddit.com/r/ExperiencedDevs/>
* **LeadDev community & events** — <https://leaddev.com/>
